Website

6out of 10

  • Reads well on a phone
  • Takes 6.8 seconds to load on mobile data. Aim for under 3.
  • No enquiry button until you scroll past the fold
  • Every trip page has dates and a day by day plan
  • No tracking on booking links, so you can't tell which post sent people

The one that matters mostYour enquiry form asks 11 questions before anyone can reach you. Name, email, trip and dates are enough to start a conversation.

Free audit, sample DMC, KathmanduPage 3 of 5

Social

5out of 10

  • 23 posts in 90 days, and 17 of them in the two weeks before your festival
  • Strong photography that is clearly your own
  • Reels reach 3 times more people than photos, but make up 1 post in 10
  • Bio link goes to the home page
  • Comments get a reply within a day

The one that matters mostPosting comes in bursts. A calendar planned a month ahead, three posts a week with one Reel, would keep you in feeds all year instead of only in festival season.

Free audit, sample DMC, KathmanduPage 4 of 5

Email

3out of 10

  • 2,400 subscribers, most of them past guests
  • Last newsletter went out 74 days ago
  • Subject lines say "Newsletter 14" instead of what's inside
  • No welcome email when someone signs up
  • The sign up form only appears on the contact page

The one that matters mostPast guests are the people most likely to book again and to send friends. One honest newsletter a month and a three email welcome would put them back to work.

Free audit, sample DMC, KathmanduPage 5 of 5

Search

4out of 10

  • First on Google for your own name
  • Not on page one for "Everest base camp trek from Kathmandu"
  • 12 Google reviews, the last one answered eight months ago
  • Every trip page shares one title, so Google can't tell them apart
  • Rated 4.8 on Tripadvisor

The one that matters mostTravellers search for the trip, not the company. A proper title on every trip page and a reply to every review is a week of work that pays for years.
