What's in the free audit?

Your website, social media, email and search, each scored out of 10 and explained in plain English. Then the three fixes worth doing first. It's written, so you can forward it to your board or owner without me in the room.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

It's free. Some people read it and ask me to do the fixes. Plenty don't, and that's fine. You won't be added to a list or chased.

Is it only social media?

No. Social builds attention, but email is where people actually book, register and join. I run both, and connect them with tracked links so you can see which posts lead to real enquiries.

How will I show my board it's working?

With one page a month. For associations that means member signups, event registrations and newsletter growth. For DMCs and operators, enquiries and bookings traced from posts and emails. For hotels, direct booking clicks against the OTAs, plus revenue from email offers.

What does it cost to work together?

It depends on how much you'd like me to take on. Social support, a full takeover and full marketing are priced differently, so we talk numbers openly once you've read your audit. You won't be paying for an agency's overheads.

You're in India and we're in the US or Europe. Will that work?

ATTA is in the US and PATA is in Bangkok, and I worked remotely with both. WFTA's network spans more than 150 countries. I plan around your working hours and reply within one working day.

Is it really just you?

Yes. I write your audit, plan the calendar, make the posts and walk you through the numbers myself, every month.
