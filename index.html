Thinking

A point of view, not a pitch.

Indian tourism keeps marketing destinations. The brands that win the next decade will market traveller transformations, what changes about the person who comes home.

The infrastructure conversation has matured. The marketing conversation has not. We pitch palaces, beaches, and Himalayas the same way we did in 2008, while the actual traveller is a 31 year old who books on Instagram, reads on Substack, and trusts no brochure.

The brands building real loyalty now are not selling destinations. They are selling who you become when you come home. Calmer, braver, better read, better fed. That is the unit of decision making for the modern traveller, and almost no one in Indian tourism is marketing to it yet.
