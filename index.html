Questions

What people ask before they send a handle.

Do you do the posting, or only advise?

I do the work. I plan, design, write, publish and reply. You approve each week before it goes live.

Are you an agency?

No. I'm one freelancer. You work with me directly, and I take a small number of tourism brands at a time.

Do you only do social media?

Most brands start there. I also write and send email newsletters, create content, edit video and help with day to day marketing, and I can look after small website updates once we work together.

You're in India and we're in the US or Europe. Will that work?

ATTA is in the US and PATA is in Bangkok, and I worked remotely with both. WFTA's network spans more than 150 countries. I plan around your working hours and reply within one working day.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many posts and platforms you need. Tell me the budget you have in mind and I'll shape the scope to fit. The free week costs nothing.

What happens after the free week?

You get the 5 posts, a short walkthrough video and a one page plan for the next 90 days. If you want me to keep going, we start the next month. If not, the posts are yours.
