Who I've worked with

  • Pacific Asia Travel Association

    A not for profit association founded in 1951 to champion responsible travel to, from and within Asia Pacific. Its members include government tourism bodies, airlines, hotels and travel companies, and its headquarters are in Bangkok.

  • Adventure Travel Trade Association

    Founded in 1990, ATTA connects the adventure travel industry around the world, from tour operators and guides to tourism boards and media. It runs the Adventure Travel World Summit and the AdventureELEVATE events.

  • Global Business Travel Association

    The trade association for business travel and meetings, founded in 1968. It represents the people who plan, buy and manage corporate travel, and brings the industry together every year at the GBTA Convention.

  • World Food Travel Association

    The nonprofit that helped define food and drink tourism as an industry of its own, founded in 2003. Through research, training and a network across 150+ countries, it links destinations, businesses and travellers who care about what they eat.

  • Adventure.travel

    ATTA's site for travellers. It helps people find and book adventure trips run by the association's member operators, from long treks to wildlife and cultural trips around the world.
