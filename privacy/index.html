What I collect, and what I do with it.

Short version. I collect what you type into a form, I count page visits, and I never sell anything to anyone.

The free week and the free audit

When you ask for the free week or an audit, your website or Instagram handle and your work email go to Formspree, which forwards them to my inbox. If you answer the three optional questions afterwards, the kind of organisation you run, your country and the budget you have in mind go the same way. I use them to write your brief or your audit, email it to you and reply if you write back. You are not added to a mailing list.

The self check

Your answers to the seven questions stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The CV and checklist forms

When you ask for my CV or the checklist, your email address goes to two places. Formspree forwards it to my inbox so I know who asked. Resend delivers the email with the file attached. I use your address to send what you asked for and to reply if you write back. You are not added to a mailing list.

Booking a call

The booking calendar is run by Cal.com. Anything you enter there, such as your name, email and notes, is collected by Cal.com under their own privacy policy and shared with me so the call can happen.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which parts of the page get read and which buttons get clicked. That involves cookies set by Google. I look at the numbers in aggregate and make no attempt to identify individual visitors.

If you visit from Europe, including the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics may not use cookies until you choose Allow in the notice at the foot of the page. If you choose No thanks, it stays that way. Anyone, anywhere, can change the choice at any time with the Cookies button at the foot of every page. No advertising cookies are ever used.

What your browser remembers

Your browser keeps three small notes for this site. Your cookie choice. Whether you closed the small offer card, so it stays closed. And any campaign tag on the link you arrived by, for that visit only. None of them identifies you, and clearing your site data removes them.

What I never do

  • Sell, rent or share your details with anyone else
  • Add you to a newsletter you did not ask for
  • Run advertising or retargeting pixels

Your choices

Email me at pratimxnarayan@gmail.com and I will delete anything you have sent me, tell you what I hold or correct it. You can block analytics cookies in your browser, or with the Cookies button, and the site works exactly the same.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page. It was last reviewed on 23 November 2026.
