What changed

Three changes, one post that works.

  1. 01

    The forest, from inside

    A kelp forest in the light replaces the murky top down shot, so the post shows the habitat the number is about.

  2. 02

    The true number leads

    17,300 plants is the fact. The bathtub maths is gone, and the area becomes sixteen tennis courts, which anyone can picture.

  3. 03

    The caption's facts on the image

    The square feet, the coast, the partner and the line that every passenger funds it moved from the caption onto the post.

The big idea. Show the forest the money grows, lead with the number that is true and big, 17,300 plants, and translate the area into something a board and a guest can picture, about sixteen tennis courts.

Style tested, Documentary and wildlife. Real moments caught as they happen, from wildlife sightings to guides and locals at work.
