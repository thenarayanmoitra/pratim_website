What changed

Three changes, one post that works.

  1. 01

    The hook is the headline

    Your flight to Barcelona is on us was buried in the caption. Now it is the biggest thing on the post, in the brand's electric blue.

  2. 02

    Barcelona, not Toronto

    The city the show is in replaces another city's stand, and the gradient wash is gone so the photo can do its job.

  3. 03

    One line of details

    The hotel, the dates and the venue sit in one line under the hook, with a button that says where to apply instead of a fake web button.

The big idea. Say the thing that gets a buyer to apply, your flight to Barcelona is on us, over the city itself, and keep the dates, the venue and the stars in one line underneath.

Style tested, Text hook overlay. Real footage with one line of text on top that makes people stop scrolling.

The numbers

One reaction for every 3,200 followers.

5 likes on 16,000 followers is 0.031% of the people who already chose to follow IBTM World. A post that lands usually hears back from about 1 in 100. That is the gap the remake is built to close, and it is the number a board understands without a chart.
