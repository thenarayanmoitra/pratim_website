What changed

Three changes, one post that works.

  1. 01

    A view only this hotel has

    The sky garden pool at Pickering replaces a spa photo that could be any hotel anywhere, and the property is named under it.

  2. 02

    The offer as one number

    25% is the biggest thing on the post after the headline. The loyalty frame became one line of small type above it.

  3. 03

    Air around the photo

    Cream margins, one serif line and the hotel's own logo, so the post looks like the hotel rather than a programme announcement.

The big idea. Name the hotel, show the garden that makes it famous, and turn the loyalty frame into one line of small type and one big number, 25%, so the offer is the easiest thing on the post to read.

Style tested, Quiet luxury. Calm frames with few or no people, where space and light do the selling.

The numbers
